Our Story

Since 2010, charity:water has been building sustainable clean water projects in Africa, South America, and Asia. We believe that everyone deserves access to clean water. It's a basic human right. Together, we're making a difference in over 29 countries and improving the lives of millions.

Real Impact: Meet Amina

Amina used to walk miles every day to collect water for her family. Thanks to your support, her village now has a clean water well. Amina can go to school and her family is healthier and happier.
