Our Mission: End the Water Crisis

charity: water is a global nonprofit organization on a mission to bring clean and safe water to every person on the planet.

We work with local partners around the world to fund sustainable water projects in communities most in need, and then we prove every project we fund using photos and GPS coordinates.

Access to clean water saves around 16,000 lives every week

Clean water opens the door to education, income, dignity, and health — especially for women and children.

Real Stories, Real Change
